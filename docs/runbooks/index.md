# Runbooks

One runbook per emergency or technical action. Each maps to a permission and carries a fork drill record before it rises above `slop` ([ADR 004](/adr/004-specifications-and-policy-as-data.md), [specification policy](/specs/specification-policy.md)). The general Lido incident process owns paging and escalation; these runbooks own the vault-specific steps and transactions.

No runbook is written yet. The table lists the runbooks that the design owes, with the permission each exercises.

| Runbook | Holder | Permission | Written | Drilled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revoke the operator | emergency Safe | `revokeTarget` and `revokeFunction` on the operator modifier and on the orders modifier, role key pinned to `operator` | No | No |
| Remove a strategy | committee's Safe, as the universal factory's trusted caller; the emergency Safe in an emergency | a motion of the universal factory that revokes the permission, or writes it again without the removed entries, with a forum post, enacted after the objection window ([ADR 006](/adr/006-governance-through-easy-track-factories.md) decision 14); in an emergency, `revokeTarget` or `revokeFunction` on the operator modifier | No | No |
| Zero the approvals | emergency Safe | `approve(spender, 0)` on each token that the operator can approve | No | No |
| Exit positions to the Safe | emergency Safe | redeem, withdraw, cancel and claim on each position, receiver and owner pinned to the avatar; the first-loss shares sit in the first-loss Safe, which this role does not reach ([ADR 008](/adr/008-funding-through-existing-payments.md)) | No | No |
| Recovery order | emergency Safe | `transfer` pinned to the orders account; then, on the orders account, a delegatecall to the CoW order signer for an order that buys USDC or USDT, pays the Aragon Agent and lives at most 1 day; a new order if it does not fill ([ADR 007](/adr/007-swapping-through-an-orders-account.md)) | No | No |
| Cancel orders and sweep the orders account | emergency Safe | on the orders account: `unsignOrder` on the order signer and `remove` on ComposableCoW for each open order, then `transfer` of each token to the Aragon Agent or the Asset Safe; where needed, an approval of CoW's vault relayer set to zero, which also stops recovery orders in that token until the restore motion raises the approval again ([ADR 007](/adr/007-swapping-through-an-orders-account.md) decision 46) | No | No |
| Return assets to the Agent | emergency Safe; or the committee's Safe, as recovery, such as the excess above the launch cap or the end of the mandate | `transfer` pinned to the Aragon Agent, with no motion and no vote; only emergency and recovery send funds back, and monitoring alerts on every transfer to the Agent ([ADR 014](/adr/014-funding-by-tranche-votes.md) decisions 8 and 9) | No | No |
| Switch the operator off | emergency Safe | `disableModule` on the Asset Safe, module argument pinned to the operator modifier; on the orders account, the revoke of the operator's targets and functions and the cancel of its open orders; for a defect in the permission layer, on the cap-breach trigger of [ADR 009](/adr/009-budgets-caps-reporting-and-monitoring.md), or when the committee is compromised or silent; revoke first where a revoke is enough ([ADR 005](/adr/005-account-graph-and-roles.md) decisions 25 and 26); then raise the universal factory's counter, so that no queued motion enacts ([ADR 013](/adr/013-one-easy-track-factory.md) decision 10) | No | No |
| Pause Easy Track | Emergency Brakes multisig | the existing Easy Track `pause()`; the multisig cannot unpause or cancel | No | No |
| Restart after a stop | committee's Safe, as the universal factory's trusted caller; Aragon Voting for an Easy Track unpause | a restart motion: after the objection window, the universal factory's restart role calls `enableModule` on the Asset Safe for the operator modifier, and restore motions bring back what the emergency role revoked, on the operator modifier ([ADR 013](/adr/013-one-easy-track-factory.md) decision 16) and on the orders account, relayer approvals included ([ADR 007](/adr/007-swapping-through-an-orders-account.md) decision 46); motions queued before the stop fail on the raised counter and are cancelled ([ADR 013](/adr/013-one-easy-track-factory.md) decision 12); an Easy Track unpause stays a vote | No | No |
| Fund a tranche | DAO | a vote with one `newImmediatePayment` from Finance to the Asset Safe for each token; the first tranche is an item of the enabling vote; the second starts only after the screening guard is set on the committee's Safe with its bypass mode off (OD-121); the proposer starts no tranche vote while a gate of [ADR 009](/adr/009-budgets-caps-reporting-and-monitoring.md) decisions 21, 36 or 41 holds (OD-122); the forum post states the funded value, the headroom under the mandate size and the treasury share ([ADR 014](/adr/014-funding-by-tranche-votes.md)) | No | No |
| Move the legacy Earn shares | the Growth Committee's Safe | `transfer` of the DAO's first-loss earnETH and earnUSD shares to the first-loss Safe, once, after the enabling vote ([ADR 008](/adr/008-funding-through-existing-payments.md)) | No | No |
| Burn first-loss Earn shares | committee's Safe, as the universal factory's trusted caller | a burn motion of the universal factory, on the Earn team's written instruction after a confirmed loss of at least 1%; below that, after a Snapshot exception; the forum post with the motion; after 72 hours without an objection, anyone enacts it, and the universal factory burns through the `first-loss-burn` role; the incident update within seven days ([ADR 008](/adr/008-funding-through-existing-payments.md) decisions 22 and 23) | No | No |
| Exit the first-loss shares | committee's Safe | the `first-loss` role's `redeem` and `claim` on the vault's redeem queue, with the proceeds pinned to the Asset Safe, on the Earn team's written instruction on a confirmed material risk; before the screening guard is set, the committee then returns what takes the vault above the first tranche to the Aragon Agent ([ADR 008](/adr/008-funding-through-existing-payments.md); [ADR 014](/adr/014-funding-by-tranche-votes.md) decision 10) | No | No |
| Unlock a matured product | committee's Safe, as the universal factory's trusted caller | a forum post that names the maturity criteria the product meets, then a budget motion that raises the product's key to the bound of an own product ([ADR 009](/adr/009-budgets-caps-reporting-and-monitoring.md)) | No | No |
| Raise the motion counter | emergency Safe; Aragon Voting | the universal factory's counter, raised with every switch, on the page for a motion that fails the check ([ADR 013](/adr/013-one-easy-track-factory.md) decision 19), and whenever an open motion must not enact; each open motion then fails at enactment, and its creator or Aragon Voting cancels it ([ADR 013](/adr/013-one-easy-track-factory.md) decision 10) | No | No |
| Run an insurance motion | committee's Safe, as the universal factory's trusted caller | a motion that creates a fund for earnETH, earnUSD or Lido Lend with the checklist of [ADR 008](/adr/008-funding-through-existing-payments.md) decision 29, moves its money from the Asset Safe to the first-loss Safe, or pays out to the fund's pinned address within its cap; a burn runs as in "Burn first-loss Earn shares" ([ADR 014](/adr/014-funding-by-tranche-votes.md) decisions 4 to 7) | No | No |
| Remove the screening guard | the owners of the committee's Safe | start the guard's 10-day removal timelock, then `setGuard` on the committee's Safe once it expires | No | No |
| Move the operator role to another Safe | DAO | a vote that grants the operator and first-loss roles to another Safe; registers a new universal factory with the new trusted caller; moves every motion role from the old factory to the new one; and removes the old factory from Easy Track (OD-80, [ADR 013](/adr/013-one-easy-track-factory.md) decision 4) | No | No |
| Rotate a signer | the committee's Safe; the emergency Safe for a seat | owner management on each Safe; a change of the committee's members needs no Snapshot each time, and monitoring alerts on every change of the owners or the threshold of the committee's Safe ([ADR 005](/adr/005-account-graph-and-roles.md) decision 33); a signer who leaves the committee or the Emergency Brakes multisig leaves the emergency seat, and that body appoints the successor; only a DAO Snapshot changes the fifth seat, and its holder must be a member of neither body ([ADR 005](/adr/005-account-graph-and-roles.md) decision 24) | No | No |
| Re-affirm open orders | the committee, through the committee's Safe | after the 30-day alert, confirm each open order or cancel it with `unsignOrder` or ComposableCoW `remove`; each monthly report lists every open order ([ADR 007](/adr/007-swapping-through-an-orders-account.md) decision 29) | No | No |
| Cancel orders on a policy change | committee's Safe, or the emergency Safe | when a policy change removes a token or tightens a bound, cancel the affected open orders, because a fill does not consult the policy ([ADR 007](/adr/007-swapping-through-an-orders-account.md)) | No | No |
| Replace the operator modifier | DAO | a vote that enables the new modifier with the compiled policy; registers a new universal factory that names it; moves every motion role to the new factory; removes the old factory from Easy Track; and rewrites the safety policy's switch and restart pins to the new modifier, all in the same action ([ADR 010](/adr/010-pre-execution-screening.md) decision 7; [ADR 013](/adr/013-one-easy-track-factory.md) decision 4) | No | No |
