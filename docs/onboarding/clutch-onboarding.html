Clutch: how the DAO's active treasury vault works

An onboarding guide for signers, engineers, reviewers and everyone who needs the picture.

Guided tour

Scene 1 of 10

The vault, and who owns it

Clutch puts a bounded part of the DAO treasury to work in approved protocols. All the assets sit in one new Safe, the Asset Safe.

The Asset Safe has exactly one owner: the Aragon Agent, the DAO's treasury. The DAO keeps custody. In an emergency, assets go back to the Agent, a fixed address.

Sources ADR 005 INV-001 brief

Scene 2 of 10

Nobody touches the vault directly

The Asset Safe switches on two Roles modifiers from Zodiac. Each holder of a role calls a modifier. The modifier checks the call against the policy and, if it is allowed, runs it as the Asset Safe.

The policy is default-deny: a call that the policy does not list is refused.

The modifiers are EIP-1167 proxies of the deployed, locked Roles mastercopy. The Asset Safe is their owner, avatar and target.

Sources ADR 005 LIP part 1

Scene 3 of 10

The operator: the committee at work

The Treasury Management Committee runs the vault through a new operator Safe, at 4 of 7. The operator can enter and leave approved protocols, within budgets.

It cannot move assets out: every receiver, owner and beneficiary in its permissions is the Asset Safe.

Scene 4 of 10

Every operator transaction is screened

[the screening vendor]'s guard sits on the operator Safe. It refuses any transaction that the vendor has not approved, once and exactly.

If the vendor stops, the operator stops too: the guard fails closed. Recovery does not change, because no guard sits on the emergency path.

Scene 5 of 10

Changes by motion, inside templates

The committee can widen its own permissions only through Easy Track motions, each built by a fixed template. LDO holders have 72 hours to object, and 0.5% of LDO stops a motion.

At enactment, the governance role writes the change. The modifier checks that write again: no role change, no delegatecall, and no administrative target. The Asset Safe and both modifiers are refused as targets.

Swaps through Stonks

Scene 7 of 10

Money in: top-ups

Funding comes from the DAO treasury through the existing payment path. Once a month, the operator Safe creates a top-up motion. LDO holders can object for 72 hours.

Two funding registries pay only the Asset Safe, each within a monthly limit. The objection is the control; the limit is the backstop.

Scene 8 of 10

Emergencies: two fast stops, one slow exit

The emergency Safe has the committee's signers at 2 of 7. Through the safety modifier it revokes the operator, exits positions and sends assets to the Aragon Agent. It is fast, but it is not independent of the committee.

The Emergency Brakes multisig, outside the committee, can switch the operator modifier off and pause Easy Track. Only the DAO is fully independent, and a DAO vote takes days.

Target: initiate any emergency action within six hours. Drills on a fork must prove it before launch.

Scene 9 of 10

Caps are watched, not enforced

No transaction reverts on a cap. Every two weeks, monitoring publishes the cap reading to IPFS. Each month, the committee publishes a report that anyone can reproduce.

If a breach is not fixed within two working days and is larger at the next reading, monitoring pages the Emergency Brakes multisig, which switches the operator off.

Scene 10 of 10

Little new code, and where it stands

Clutch reuses audited code that already runs: Safe, Zodiac Roles, Easy Track, Stonks and Lido's monitoring. It adds Easy Track factories, the permission policy with its compiler, the vault's detectors and a report generator.

Today the design is in phase 0. Nothing is deployed, no audit is done and no runbook is written. The Built or reused and Status tabs show each part.

◐ Specified, not built

How the vault swaps one token for another. It never trades on CoW directly. It sends the token to a swap instance whose receiver is fixed to the Asset Safe.

Trigger The committee wants a different mix of assets, for example less stETH and more USDC. Time One order lasts up to 1800 seconds.

Why it matters

The Asset Safe owns both modifiers, and every role call runs as the Asset Safe. The emergency revoke and the governance writes work only because a narrow role may administer a modifier. The LIP calls this composition the primary risk.
