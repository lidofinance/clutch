# Decision records

One global sequence. Every record is agent-drafted and `proposed` until EM accepts it. The Treasury Management Committee is the permanent acceptor ([ADR 002](002-decision-and-review-process.md)). Since 2026-10-13 no other body verifies a record before it is accepted (OD-63). Open items are in the [open-decisions register](/registers/open-decisions.md).

# Repository and process

* [ADR 001: Repository scope, visibility, licence and name](001-repository-scope-visibility-licence-name.md) - One repository for the Active Treasury system, private until deployment at the latest, AGPL-3.0-or-later with GPL-3.0 kept for files derived from Easy Track and LGPL-3.0-only for files derived from the policy provider's constellation, named Clutch.
* [ADR 002: Decision and review process](002-decision-and-review-process.md) - EM accepts decisions in the interim and the Treasury Management Committee permanently; no other body verifies an ADR, since the Emergency Brakes multisig's verification ended on 2026-10-13; pages carry OKF 0.2 trust fields and a review ladder that an edit resets.
* [ADR 003: Agent operating model](003-agent-operating-model.md) - AGENTS.md is the single source of truth, at most 60 lines of rules, capped by the validator; skills hold procedures; agents never merge, never touch deployed records and never set parameters.
* [ADR 004: Specifications and the permission policy as data](004-specifications-and-policy-as-data.md) - Hand-written conceptual specs, a generated API reference, invariants mapped to tests, the LIP as an external layer, runbooks as their own class, and a permission policy written as a Zodiac constellation that Clutch's own compiler turns into one committed JSON artifact; the team works in the Zodiac UI, local tooling verifies everything the UI produces, and a round-trip check compares the chain with the artifact.
* [ADR 012: Contributing with many agents](012-contributing-with-many-agents.md) - Many contributors may run their own agents here — any agent may author a page, and only listed humans and teams verify, with a hash that locks the page's body; agents own no GitHub accounts; changes land through a merge queue; decisions enter as complete open-decision rows and are decided in batched interviews; safe defaults, one command for setup, checks and generated files, and checks for restricted terms, licences, invariants and IDs; the onboarding guide is checked in every presentation state, and each of its items is pinned to the decisions that it cites.

# System design

* [ADR 005: Account graph and roles](005-account-graph-and-roles.md) - A Safe owned only by Aragon Voting holds the assets; two Roles modifiers split the operator and governance roles from the emergency role; the committee's own Safe holds the operator role; the emergency Safe, three of five with two committee members, two Emergency Brakes members and one member of neither body, holds the emergency role and its switch; the Emergency Brakes multisig touches no funds and keeps only the Easy Track pause; no emergency body resumes what it stopped; a Voting-owned orders account with one modifier of its own places every CoW order, and a Voting-owned first-loss Safe holds the first-loss shares, which burn only by Easy Track motion and redeem to the Agent through one narrow role of the committee's Safe; the Asset Safe, the committee's Safe and the emergency Safe run Safe v1.5.0, and the Asset Safe has no fallback handler.
* [ADR 006: Governance through Easy Track factories](006-governance-through-easy-track-factories.md) - Easy Track factories are the only new contracts; the committee's own Safe is their trusted caller; each factory builds a permission tree from a template fixed at audit time, so a motion carries parameters, never a tree; the motion that onboards a protocol also adds its emergency exit through an exit-governance role on the safety modifier; a routine removal is a removal-template motion, and an emergency removal is the emergency Safe's immediate revoke.
* [ADR 007: Swapping through an orders account](007-swapping-through-an-orders-account.md) - Every swap, routine or emergency, is a CoW order placed from a dedicated orders account — a Safe owned by Aragon Voting, with CoW's fallback handler and one modifier of its own; the operator funds it under budgets and places market, limit and TWAP orders that pay the Asset Safe; the emergency role places recovery orders that pay the Aragon Agent into USDC or USDT, or sends assets to the Agent; the launch uses CoW only; Stonks 2.0 is no longer used; staking stays, and the withdrawal queue left the design on 2026-10-08.
* [ADR 008: Funding through the existing payment path](008-funding-through-existing-payments.md) - The vault is seeded and topped up through Aragon Finance and two dedicated allowed-recipients registries, one for stablecoins and one for stETH, each with a one-month period and a limit of one TM Floor Value; the objection is the control and the registry is the backstop; a DAO vote adds USDS to the shared payment permission and to the shared stablecoin token list; ETH is not a funding asset; the DAO's first-loss Earn shares move from the Growth Committee into a dedicated first-loss Safe, where a burn runs only as an Easy Track motion and one narrow role of the committee's Safe redeems them to the Aragon Agent, on the Earn team's instruction, and they reduce the seed; until the screening guard is live, the committee keeps the vault under a cap of one million dollars by procedure.
* [ADR 009: Budgets, exposure caps, reporting and monitoring](009-budgets-caps-reporting-and-monitoring.md) - Every mandate rule gets a control on each layer that can carry it — on-chain policy, screening, monitoring, display — mapped in a control matrix; exposure caps and the liquidity buffer are detected, reported on IPFS, screened where the vendor can check them, and shown in the Zodiac UI from Lido's readings and alerts; prices follow a five-level waterfall, so no asset is unpriced; a new Lido product stays in the protocol cap until it matures and a motion unlocks it; budgets derive from the mandate by attested computation, are set once, and change by motion only after an event; detectors run in two monitoring estates; the committee owns remediation.
* [ADR 010: Pre-execution screening](010-pre-execution-screening.md) - A suspicious operator transaction is blocked on chain before it executes, by the screening vendor's existing transaction guard on the Safe that holds the operator role, the committee's own Safe since OD-80, which is also the trusted caller of every factory; it fails closed, and recovery never passes through vendor code; the committee's Safe runs Safe v1.3.0, which the vendor's guard and Lido's detector support, and the vendor is asked to refuse delegatecalls except to Safe's batching library. The vault may launch without the guard under a cap of one million dollars, held by procedure; a forum post lifts the cap once the guard is live.
* [ADR 011: Launch scope](011-launch-scope.md) - The launch assets and venues; Lido Lend is onboarded by motion through a Morpho Blue template that ships at launch with end-to-end tests, and stays in the protocol cap until it matures; swaps are CoW orders from the orders account; DAI earns through Sky's DAI–USDS converter; sDAI, third-party lending markets, and router swaps on Uniswap and 1inch are out at launch.
